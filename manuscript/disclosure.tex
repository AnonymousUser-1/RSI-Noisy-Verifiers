\subsection*{AI use statement}

We used generative AI tools, including OpenAI Codex and Claude-based agents,
to assist with the conceptual framework, hypotheses, mathematical claims and
proofs, experimental design, implementation, synthetic-task generators, and
analytical tests. AI assistance also supported literature retrieval and
synthesis, research coordination and internal review, manuscript organization
and editing, LaTeX preparation, and reference formatting.

For the empirical analysis, these tools assisted with provenance checks,
aggregation of saved measurements, figures and tables, and interpretation
of the findings and limitations. The reported measurements come from
experimental records, not AI-generated values; this assistance does not replace
independent validation of raw responses. The authors reviewed the AI-assisted
claims, proofs, experimental design and implementation, citations, novelty
claims, and manuscript text, and take responsibility for the final content.
